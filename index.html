Good design is

I’m Kamaldeen, a front-end developer with a soft spot for clear systems, expressive details, and the moment an interface just clicks.

I work at the point where design meets code. That means I can take an idea from a rough sketch to a polished, production-ready interface, and I care about the parts most people never notice: load times, keyboard navigation, and how a page behaves on a cheap phone with a weak signal.
